Commercial Lighting Intelligence Platform

Challenge

Preparing a commercial lighting quote can mean pulling customer requirements, product details, supplier responses, pricing, freight, lead times, and margin information from different places before a salesperson can make a decision.

What we built

One workspace that brings the information needed to evaluate supplier options and prepare the customer quote together.

  • Customer requests and product requirements
  • Supplier pricing, freight, and lead times
  • Product options and missing information
  • Margin visibility and proposal preparation

Why it matters

Salespeople can compare the information they need in one place instead of searching through emails, files, and systems before they can move the work forward.
