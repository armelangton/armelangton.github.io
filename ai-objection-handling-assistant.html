Challenge

A remote managed IT, cybersecurity, and compliance sales team had to learn how to handle technical objections consistently without having a manager sitting beside them. Reps regularly heard objections such as “we already have an IT provider,” “we handle IT internally,” “we’re all set,” “send me information,” or “I’m too busy,” and the right response depended on understanding the prospect’s environment rather than simply pushing past the objection.

What we built

An AI objection-handling coach that gave reps a place to practice realistic prospect conversations and reinforced the same discovery approach, messaging, and coaching they were learning during onboarding. It was also available at their fingertips during live calls when they needed help thinking through the next question or response.

  • Realistic managed IT, cybersecurity, and compliance objection scenarios
  • Suggested responses that acknowledged the objection before moving back into discovery
  • Next questions focused on security, compliance, governance, ownership, timing, and operational triggers
  • Conversation goals, CRM note suggestions, and short coaching prompts to reinforce the reason behind the response

Why it matters

The coach gave a distributed team a consistent way to practice between live coaching sessions and reduced the need to rely on memory, scattered training materials, or immediate manager availability. During calls, reps could quickly get guidance that helped them keep the conversation moving without turning every objection into a scripted rebuttal.
