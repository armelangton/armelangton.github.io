AI Guidance Assistant

Challenge

Employees often need useful guidance while they are handling a question, exception, decision, or next step, but the information they need may be spread across documents, notes, training, and internal knowledge.

What we built

An AI assistant designed to surface relevant guidance for the business situation in front of the user.

  • Context-aware guidance based on the situation
  • Suggested responses or actions
  • Questions that help clarify what matters
  • Recommended next steps using approved business knowledge

Why it matters

People can get focused help while they are doing the work instead of relying only on memory or searching across multiple sources for the right information.
