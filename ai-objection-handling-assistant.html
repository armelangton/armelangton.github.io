AI Sales Coaching Assistant

Challenge

A remote technical sales team needed a better way to practice objection handling and reinforce training without relying on a manager being available in the moment. Reps also needed quick access to the right guidance while they were actively on a customer call.

What we built

An AI coaching assistant that gave the team a practical way to rehearse common objections and pull up focused guidance at their fingertips during live conversations.

  • Practice scenarios built around common technical sales objections
  • Suggested responses and follow-up questions for the situation
  • Coaching prompts that reinforced the team’s training and messaging
  • Fast access to next-step guidance while a rep was on a call

Why it matters

The remote team could practice more consistently, reinforce objection-handling skills between coaching sessions, and get useful support in the moment without searching through training materials or waiting for someone else to respond.
