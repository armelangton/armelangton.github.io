What Palomma does

Practical systems that connect the work behind your business.

Palomma Consulting helps small and mid-sized businesses improve the systems, workflows, and decisions behind daily operations. We design AI assistants, workflow automations, and connected business applications, then integrate them with the tools teams already use, including CRM, documents, knowledge systems, reporting, and internal processes.

The result is practical infrastructure that reduces repetitive work, improves handoffs, strengthens visibility, and gives teams better support while keeping people in control where judgment and approval matter.
