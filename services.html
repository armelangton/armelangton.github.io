What Palomma does

Connected business technology built around real operations.

Palomma Consulting helps small and mid-sized businesses improve the systems, workflows, and decisions behind daily operations. We design AI assistants, workflow automations, and connected business applications, then integrate them with the tools teams already use.

The result is less repetitive work, cleaner handoffs, better visibility, and stronger support for the people making decisions.
