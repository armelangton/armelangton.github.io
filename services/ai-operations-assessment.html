Common questions

What if we know something is wrong but do not know what is causing it?

That is a good reason to start here. We look at how the work happens, how the systems support it, where the problems are coming from, and what needs to change.

Can you improve software we already use instead of replacing it?

Yes. The answer may be better configuration, cleaner data, integrations, automation, changes to the process, or a combination of those rather than replacing the system.

Does the solution have to include AI?

No. AI is used when it improves the work. The right answer may instead be process changes, automation, integrations, better use of an existing system, or a new application.
