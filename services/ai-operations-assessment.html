Common questions

What if we know something is wrong but do not know what is causing it?

That is a good reason to start here. We look at what people are trying to accomplish, how the work currently moves, what the systems are doing, where information comes from, and where time or reliability is being lost. Then we determine what actually needs to change.

Can you fix an implementation another company or employee started?

Potentially, yes. We first determine what is working, what is incomplete or unreliable, and whether the existing implementation is worth correcting. The answer may be to finish it, simplify it, replace part of it, or take a different approach.

Can you improve software we already use instead of replacing it?

Yes. Better configuration, cleaner information, improved reporting, integrations, automation, training, or changes to the process may solve the problem without replacing the system.

What happens if the problem turns out to need a different Palomma solution?

We use what we learn to define the right next step. That may be a focused automation, AI agent, business application, integration, system improvement, or a combination rather than continuing with work the business does not need.

Does this have to include AI?

No. AI is one option. We use it when it improves the result; we do not add it simply because a project involves business improvement.
