Business Process Improvement

Fix the work before adding more technology to it. Palomma examines how an important process actually moves across people, systems, information, decisions, and handoffs; identifies what is creating friction; and helps redesign and implement a better way of working.

How this service is scoped

Scope depends on how broad the process is, how many teams and systems are involved, the amount of redesign needed, and whether the engagement ends with a redesigned process or continues into configuration, automation, integration, application, or AI implementation.

When this is a good fit

  • Everyone knows a process is inefficient, but the real cause is unclear
  • Work contains too many handoffs, approvals, duplicate steps, or manual checks
  • Employees rely on tribal knowledge because the process is not clearly defined
  • Technology has been added over time without simplifying the underlying work
  • Errors, delays, or rework keep appearing in the same part of the process
  • You are considering AI or automation but need to know what should actually change first

How the engagement works

  1. 1

    Observe the real process

    Review what people actually do, not only what the documented process says.

  2. 2

    Find the causes

    Separate symptoms from the underlying process, information, ownership, or system problems.

  3. 3

    Design the better process

    Simplify the flow and define what should change, stay manual, or be supported by technology.

  4. 4

    Implement the improvement

    Put the changes into practice through process, system, automation, AI, or enablement work as agreed.

Examples

Reduce repeated information gathering, clarify supplier and customer handoffs, improve exceptions, and connect the work to the systems already in use.

Customer onboarding

What affects scope

  • Number of teams, roles, and handoffs involved
  • How much of the end-to-end process is included
  • System and data complexity
  • Amount of documentation or standardization needed
  • Whether implementation is included after redesign
  • Change, training, and adoption requirements

Common questions

Is this just an assessment?

No. The service can include analysis and redesign only when that is what the client needs, but it can also continue into implementation. The point is to improve the process, not to sell an assessment as a required first step.

Do you automate the process after redesigning it?

When automation is the right answer and it is included in the agreed scope, yes. Some improvements require automation; others require configuration, integration, clearer ownership, better information, training, or no new technology at all.

Can this be done without AI?

Yes. Palomma is an AI specialist, but AI should not be forced into a process that has a simpler and better solution.

How is this different from Workflow Automation?

Process Improvement starts with understanding and redesigning how the work should operate. Workflow Automation is focused on implementing the connected flow of steps, systems, actions, and approvals once that process is sufficiently clear.

Know the process is broken but not sure what to change?

Start with the work itself. We will help identify what is creating the friction and what kind of improvement will actually solve it.
