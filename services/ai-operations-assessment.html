Business Process Improvement

Improve work that is slow, inconsistent, overly manual, or difficult to manage. We examine how the process works today, redesign what needs to change, and determine whether better process, system configuration, automation, integration, AI, or a custom application is the right answer.

How This Work Is Scoped

Scope depends on the process breadth, teams involved, systems and information sources, number of handoffs and decisions, and whether Palomma is analyzing, redesigning, implementing, or doing a combination of that work.

What This Can Include

▤

Current-State Process Review

Understand how work enters, moves, gets reviewed, changes hands, and gets completed across people, systems, documents, and decisions.

!

Friction and Bottleneck Analysis

Identify delays, duplicate work, unnecessary steps, missing information, unclear ownership, rework, and recurring failure points.

◎

Roles and Decision Review

Clarify who owns each stage, decision, handoff, approval, exception, and next action.

⇄

Systems and Information Review

Evaluate where information lives, how it moves, which tools are helping, and where disconnected systems create extra work.

→

Future-State Process Design

Design a simpler process with clearer steps, responsibilities, required information, decisions, and exception paths.

✦

AI and Automation Design

Determine where AI, automation, integrations, configuration, or applications can improve the redesigned process—and where they should not be used.

▦

Implementation

When appropriate, Palomma can implement the agreed process, system, automation, integration, or AI changes rather than stopping at recommendations.

✓

Documentation and Adoption

Document the improved process and provide the guidance people need to work with it consistently.

What Changes

  • People have a clearer understanding of how the work should move
  • Delays, rework, and unnecessary handoffs are reduced
  • Ownership and decision points are easier to manage
  • Technology choices are tied to the actual business problem
  • AI and automation are used where they create practical value
  • Improvement can continue into implementation instead of ending with a report
