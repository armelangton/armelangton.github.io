Good fit when

  • Work regularly gets stuck or delayed
  • People have created extra steps or workarounds just to get the job done
  • It is unclear who owns a step or what should happen next
  • The same work is done differently depending on who does it
  • You know something is not working, but you do not know what is causing the problem

Process improvement may not be the right fit when

  • The process already works and you only want to remove repetitive manual steps — Workflow Automation may be a better option
  • The process already works and you only need information to move between systems — Integrations & Data may be a better option
  • You already know exactly what software needs to be built — Business Applications may be a better option

Common questions

What if we know something is wrong but do not know what is causing it?

That is a good reason to start here. We look at how the work actually happens, find what is creating the problem, and determine what should change.

Is this just an assessment?

No. We can identify the problem, redesign the process, and help put the changes in place.

Does the solution have to include AI or automation?

No. The right answer could be changing the process, using AI or automation, connecting systems, improving existing software, building something new, or combining several of those.
