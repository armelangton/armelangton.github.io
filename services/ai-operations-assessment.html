Examples of a Good Fit

  • Lead routing and handoff
  • Opportunity stages and approval flow
  • Quote creation and approval
  • Sales-to-operations handoff
  • Pipeline review and forecasting

Scope

  • One clearly defined sales or revenue process
  • One primary team or business function
  • Input from up to three people directly involved in the process
  • One current-state map and one future-state workflow design
  • One consolidated round of factual corrections or minor refinements

Implementation Is Separate
