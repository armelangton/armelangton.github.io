Quick answer

Use this when the problem is broader than one task or workflow.

An AI & Operations Assessment examines the workflows, systems, handoffs, and decisions surrounding a business problem. The goal is to identify what is actually creating friction, where AI or automation may help, and what should be addressed first.

You leave with a practical roadmap whether or not Palomma completes the implementation.

Good fit when

The issue crosses systems, people, or processes.

  • You see repeated delays, rework, or manual handoffs but the root cause is unclear.
  • Several tools or teams are involved in the same process.
  • You are considering AI or automation but do not want to start with the technology.
  • You need priorities, scope, and a practical implementation path before making a larger investment.

What can be included

A focused review of the operation around the problem.

Current-state review

Understand how the work moves today, including systems, inputs, outputs, owners, and handoffs.

Stakeholder input

Gather context from the people closest to the work and surface gaps between the intended and actual process.

Opportunity analysis

Identify bottlenecks, duplicated effort, missing information, automation opportunities, and decision-support needs.

Technology considerations

Evaluate where existing tools, configuration changes, integrations, AI, or custom development may fit.

Typical deliverables

Clear recommendations you can act on.

  • 01
    Current-state findings

    A concise view of the process, systems, and friction identified during the assessment.

  • 02
    Prioritized opportunities

    Recommendations ranked by expected value, effort, dependencies, and risk.

  • 03
    Recommended solution path

    A practical direction for process, automation, AI, integrations, or custom development.

  • 04
    Implementation roadmap

    Suggested phases, sequencing, assumptions, and next steps.

Scope and pricing

The standard assessment begins at $2,500.

The starting price is intended for a clearly defined operational area with a limited number of workflows, systems, and stakeholder interviews.

Additional departments, locations, systems, data analysis, workshops, or documentation requirements may change the scope. The complete fixed fee is agreed before work begins.
