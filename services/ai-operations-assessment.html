Service package

AI & Operations Assessment

Map the workflows, systems, handoffs, and decisions around a business problem before investing in the solution.

Quick answer

This assessment is for businesses that know something is slowing the work down but need clarity on the root cause, the best opportunity, and what should improve first. You receive a practical roadmap and fixed-scope implementation direction whether or not Palomma completes the build.

Why this matters

Start with the operation, not the technology.

AI and automation create value when they are connected to a real workflow, supported by usable information, and placed where they reduce friction or improve decisions. The assessment identifies where that opportunity exists before a larger implementation begins.

Who it is for

Businesses with repeated delays, manual handoffs, disconnected systems, inconsistent information, or unclear ownership that need a practical direction before investing in a workflow, AI agent, automation, or custom application.

What we review

  • How work enters, moves, gets approved, and gets handed off today
  • The systems, tools, spreadsheets, documents, and data involved
  • Where information is missing, duplicated, delayed, or difficult to trust
  • Where AI, automation, configuration, integration, or process changes may help
  • The risks, dependencies, permissions, and human decisions that must remain controlled

What you receive

Current-state map

A clear view of the workflow, systems, inputs, outputs, owners, and handoffs involved.

Opportunity analysis

Friction points and improvement opportunities ranked by expected value, effort, dependency, and risk.

Recommended path

Practical direction for process, automation, AI, integrations, configuration, or custom development.

Implementation roadmap

Suggested sequencing, assumptions, next steps, and a fixed-fee proposal when the work is clear enough to scope.

Typical assessment path

  1. 1
    Understand the current operation

    Review the business need, stakeholders, workflow, systems, information, and known friction.

  2. 2
    Identify and rank the opportunities

    Separate symptoms from root causes and determine where the greatest practical value exists.

  3. 3
    Define the recommended next step

    Document the roadmap, boundaries, dependencies, timeline, and implementation options.

Common questions

What is included in the assessment?

The standard scope includes a defined operational area, process and system review, up to two stakeholder interviews, opportunity analysis, prioritized recommendations, and a 90-day improvement roadmap.

Do we need clean data before starting?

No. The assessment identifies what information is usable, what may need cleanup, and where better structure or human review is needed.

Will the assessment include an implementation proposal?

Yes, when the recommended work is clear enough to scope responsibly. The proposal defines the fixed fee, deliverables, assumptions, and expected timeline.

Can you review systems or AI tools we already use?

Yes. Existing CRM, automation, AI assistants, integrations, workflows, reporting, permissions, and failure points can be included within the agreed scope.
