Sales Operations & CRM Workflows

Improve the work around your CRM so teams spend less time searching, updating, and coordinating routine sales work.

What this solves

Sales teams often work across CRM, email, documents, research sources, tasks, and internal resources. Palomma can connect those pieces around a defined workflow so information is easier to find, routine updates happen more consistently, and people get the context they need at the right point in the process.

Common examples

Account preparation

Gather approved account, contact, activity, and internal context before a meeting or sales task.

CRM updates

Prepare or automate routine record updates, task creation, status changes, and handoffs.

Sales research and support

Bring together relevant information and approved resources without forcing reps to search across systems.

Follow-through

Coordinate next steps, reminders, internal tasks, and reviewed customer-facing preparation.

How the workflow can work

  1. 1
    Sales work is triggered

    A new account, stage change, meeting, task, renewal event, or other CRM activity starts the workflow.

  2. 2
    Context is assembled

    Relevant CRM records, notes, approved documents, or connected-system information is gathered.

  3. 3
    AI prepares support

    AI can summarize, research, organize, draft, or recommend using the approved context and instructions.

  4. 4
    Rules and actions run

    Tasks, record updates, routing, reminders, and deterministic workflow steps move the process forward.

  5. 5
    People review and act

    Reps, managers, or operations owners remain responsible for consequential sales decisions and customer commitments.

What can be included

  • CRM record preparation and updates
  • Account and contact research support
  • Meeting preparation
  • Internal knowledge retrieval
  • Task and handoff automation
  • Opportunity or account review preparation
  • Drafting support using approved context
  • Manager review or approval points

What a finished implementation looks like

A working production workflow connected to the agreed sales systems and responsibilities, with appropriate controls, testing, documentation, handoff, and bounded stabilization. The solution is designed around the actual sales process rather than adding AI for its own sake.
