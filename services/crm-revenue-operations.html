Good fit when

  • Your CRM does not match the way the team works
  • People avoid or work around the CRM
  • Pipeline or reporting is hard to trust
  • Follow-up and handoffs are inconsistent
  • Sales knowledge lives in too many places
  • Reps spend too much time on admin instead of customers

CRM & RevOps work may not be the right fit when

  • The real problem is outside the customer or revenue process
  • The underlying sales process is not defined yet
  • The issue is only a single disconnected system
  • A simple training change would solve the problem
  • The team will not use or maintain the agreed process
  • Replacing the CRM is assumed before the current one is understood

Common questions

Do we need to replace our CRM?

Usually not. We first look at what is not working and whether the system you already have can be improved.

Can you help beyond CRM configuration?

Yes. This can include customer and sales workflows, reporting, automation, handoffs, playbooks, onboarding, and other sales enablement needs.

Can AI be part of our CRM or revenue process?

Yes, when it can reduce admin, surface useful information, support decisions, or help people complete customer work.
