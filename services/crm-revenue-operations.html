CRM & Revenue Operations

Improve the systems and processes behind customer and revenue work so CRM, pipeline, handoffs, reporting, guidance, and follow-through support the way the business actually operates. AI and automation can be added where they create value, but the work starts with making the underlying revenue system more useful.

How This Work Is Scoped

Scope depends on the CRM and tools involved, process breadth, data quality, reporting needs, integrations, automation, user adoption, and the number of teams or lifecycle stages affected.

What This Can Include

◇

Revenue Process Design

Clarify lifecycle stages, opportunity stages, qualification, handoffs, entry and exit criteria, and required next actions.

▦

CRM Configuration and Redesign

Improve fields, layouts, views, objects, workflows, and user experience so the CRM better supports real work.

◎

Ownership and Handoffs

Define who owns leads, opportunities, customers, renewals, tasks, and exceptions as work moves through the revenue lifecycle.

✓

Data Standards and Quality

Standardize critical information, sources of truth, required fields, duplicate handling, and practical maintenance rules.

▤

Reporting and Forecasting

Build useful visibility into pipeline health, movement, conversion, activity, renewals, risk, and outcomes.

→

Automation and Follow-Through

Create routing, tasks, reminders, alerts, status changes, and next-step workflows that reduce manual coordination.

✦

AI-Assisted Revenue Work

Add research, summaries, drafting, recommendations, data preparation, coaching, or other AI-supported work where appropriate.

↗

Enablement and Adoption

Provide definitions, guidance, job aids, training, and in-workflow support so people understand and use the improved system consistently.

What Changes

  • CRM and revenue processes are easier to understand and use
  • Ownership and handoffs are clearer
  • Pipeline and reporting become more reliable
  • Missing information and stalled work become visible sooner
  • Automation reduces repetitive follow-through
  • Enablement is connected to the systems and processes people use every day
