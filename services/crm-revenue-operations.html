CRM & Revenue Operations

Make the systems behind customer and revenue work easier to use, easier to trust, and better connected to the way your team actually operates. Palomma can improve CRM structure, process, data, reporting, automation, enablement, and AI without assuming the answer is a brand-new platform.

How this service is scoped

Scope depends on the CRM or systems involved, process complexity, data condition, reporting needs, automation and integrations, user adoption issues, and whether the work is a focused improvement or a broader revenue-system redesign.

When this is a good fit

  • Your CRM does not reflect the way the team actually sells or serves customers
  • Pipeline stages, ownership, handoffs, or next steps are inconsistent
  • Reports are hard to trust because the underlying data or process is inconsistent
  • Salespeople spend too much time updating records, searching for context, or managing reminders
  • Important activity happens outside the CRM and never becomes visible to the rest of the team
  • You want to add AI or automation but the underlying process and data need improvement first

Common questions

Do we need to replace our CRM?

Usually not. The first question is whether the existing CRM can support the process better through configuration, cleanup, integration, and workflow improvement. Replacement only makes sense when the current platform is truly the constraint.

Can you work with Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho?

Yes. Palomma can work with existing CRM platforms and the surrounding systems, subject to the capabilities and access available in the customer's environment.

Is this only for sales teams?

No. CRM and revenue operations often span marketing intake, sales, customer onboarding, account management, renewals, service handoffs, and leadership reporting.

Where does sales enablement fit now?

Enablement is included when the CRM or revenue-system change requires better guidance, playbooks, onboarding, training, manager support, or resources inside the workflow. It does not need to be sold as a separate top-level service.

Is the CRM supporting the work—or creating more of it?

Show us where the process breaks down. We will help determine whether the right answer is configuration, process redesign, automation, AI, integration, or a combination.
