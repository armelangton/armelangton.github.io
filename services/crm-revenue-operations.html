Usually not. We first look at whether the current system can be improved through better process, configuration, data, and integrations.

Can you work with Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho?

Yes, subject to the capabilities and access available in your environment.

Where does sales enablement fit?

It is included when better guidance, onboarding, playbooks, or training are needed to make the CRM and revenue process work better.
