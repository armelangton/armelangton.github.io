Common questions

What can an AI agent actually do for my business?

It can work with information, analyze and compare, prepare or create work, make recommendations, and complete defined actions where appropriate.

Can it work with the tools and information we already use?

Often, yes. What it can connect to depends on the systems, access, and information available.

Do I need to know exactly what kind of AI agent I need?

No. Start with the work or problem. We can determine whether AI, automation, process improvement, or another approach makes the most sense.
