Common questions

Does an agent have to act on its own?

No. It can assist, recommend, prepare work, or act only where you want it to.

Can it use our existing systems and information?

Usually, yes, when those sources can be accessed reliably and securely.

What happens when it is unsure?

It can stop, ask, or route the work to a person instead of guessing.
