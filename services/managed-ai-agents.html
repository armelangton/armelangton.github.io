AI Agents & Assistants

AI built around a real business job — answering, comparing, preparing, recommending, or taking a defined action.

Good fit when

  • Repetitive information work takes too much time
  • Answers depend on your business context
  • AI can help while people stay in control

Common questions

Does an agent have to act on its own?

No. It can recommend, prepare, or act only after approval.

Can it use our existing systems and documents?

Usually, yes, when those sources can be accessed reliably and securely.

What happens when it is unsure?

It can stop, ask, or route the work to a person instead of guessing.
