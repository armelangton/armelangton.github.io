Common questions

Does an agent have to act on its own?

No. It can recommend, prepare, or act only after approval.

Can it use our existing systems and documents?

Usually, yes, when those sources can be accessed reliably and securely.

What happens when it is unsure?

We design a clear exception path so it can stop, ask, or route the work to a person instead of guessing.
