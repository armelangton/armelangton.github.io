AI Agents & Assistants

Build AI that does more than chat. Palomma creates focused agents and assistants that can understand approved business information, answer questions, compare options, prepare work, make recommendations, and take defined actions inside the systems your team already uses.

How this service is scoped

Scope depends on what the AI needs to understand, which systems it must use, how much judgment or autonomy is appropriate, how often it runs, and what Palomma continues to operate after launch.

When this is a good fit

  • People spend too much time reading, summarizing, comparing, researching, or preparing information
  • Employees repeatedly answer the same questions or search across scattered documents and systems
  • A business task follows clear rules but still requires judgment, context, or language understanding
  • Your team needs better recommendations or prepared next steps before a person makes the final decision
  • You want AI to take defined actions while keeping human review where it matters
  • You need an AI capability that works with your actual business information rather than a generic chatbot

Common questions

Does an AI agent have to act on its own?

No. Many useful agents prepare information, make recommendations, or draft the next action while a person remains responsible for approval. Autonomy should match the risk and the business need.

Can it use the systems and documents we already have?

Usually, yes. The solution can be designed around approved documents, CRM or ERP data, email, forms, databases, and other systems when those sources can be accessed reliably and securely.

What happens when the AI is unsure?

We design explicit exception paths. Depending on the use case, the agent can ask for missing information, flag uncertainty, stop the action, or route the case to a person rather than inventing an answer.

What does the ongoing monthly service cover?

That depends on the operating model agreed for the solution. It may include monitoring, maintenance, compatibility updates, troubleshooting, and support for the implemented capability. Third-party usage or software costs may be separate.
