AI Agents & Assistants

Build AI around a defined business job using your approved information, business rules, and existing systems. Agents and assistants can answer questions, analyze information, prepare recommendations, complete defined actions, or help people move work forward with the right controls in place.

How This Work Is Scoped

Scope depends on the business job, information sources, systems involved, level of autonomy, review requirements, usage, and the operational responsibility Palomma retains after launch.

What This Can Include

◎

Knowledge Assistants

Answer questions from approved policies, procedures, documents, product information, or other business knowledge.

▤

Information Analysis

Extract, organize, compare, classify, summarize, and reconcile information from emails, documents, spreadsheets, and systems.

✦

Recommendations and Decision Support

Prepare options, identify differences or exceptions, and surface the information a person needs to make a decision.

→

Defined Actions

Create records, prepare updates, route work, trigger approved next steps, or take other bounded actions where appropriate.

✓

Human Review and Approval

Keep people in control when judgment, authorization, customer communication, or business authority is required.

⇄

Existing-System Connections

Work with CRM, ERP, email, documents, calendars, knowledge sources, and other systems already used by the business.

!

Exceptions and Fallbacks

Make uncertainty, missing information, failures, and unusual cases visible with clear paths for human handling.

◉

Testing and Evaluation

Test representative scenarios, define acceptable behavior, document controls, and evaluate performance before and after launch.

What Changes

  • People spend less time searching for and preparing information
  • Routine analysis and defined actions can happen more consistently
  • Exceptions and missing information become easier to spot
  • Business knowledge is easier to use in the flow of work
  • Human decisions remain visible where they matter
  • AI is connected to the actual business job rather than added as a separate tool
