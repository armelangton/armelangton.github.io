AI Agents & Assistants

Add capacity for work that takes time because someone has to find information, understand it, compare it, analyze it, or create something from it.

Starting at $1,500+

Possibilities

⌕

Find and bring together information

Pull useful information from documents, messages, systems, or other approved sources so people do not have to search for it themselves.

⇄

Compare and analyze

Review options, records, requests, products, suppliers, or other business information and point out what matters.

✦

Prepare work

Turn the information it finds into summaries, drafts, recommendations, responses, or other work for a person to review or use.

Good fit when

  • People spend a lot of time searching through documents, messages, or systems to find what they need
  • Someone has to read, compare, summarize, or make sense of a large amount of information
  • The same type of research, analysis, or preparation happens again and again
  • Important knowledge is concentrated in a few people, creating delays when others need answers
  • Your team needs more capacity, but the work still requires understanding information rather than simply following fixed steps

Another solution may be a better fit when

  • The work follows the same clear steps every time and mainly needs to happen automatically — Workflow Automation may be a better option
  • People need a new place to manage the work, records, or decisions — a Business Application may be a better option
  • The process or system itself is causing the problem and you are not yet sure what should change — Systems & Process Improvement may be a better place to start
  • AI would be expected to make a final safety, legal, financial, compliance, employment, or other high-consequence decision without a person reviewing it
