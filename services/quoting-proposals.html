Quoting & Proposals

Improve the work between an incoming request and a reviewed quote or proposal.

What this solves

Quoting often depends on information spread across emails, documents, product data, pricing sources, CRM records, and individual experience. Palomma can build a connected workflow that gathers the right inputs, helps interpret requirements, prepares the work, and keeps human review where it matters.

Common examples

RFQ intake

Capture requests, identify missing information, and organize requirements before work begins.

Quote preparation

Bring together product, service, pricing, availability, and customer information for review.

Proposal drafting

Prepare consistent first drafts using approved information, templates, and business rules.

Review and approval

Route exceptions, pricing decisions, and final documents to the right person before release.

How the workflow can work

  1. 1
    Request received

    Email, form, file, CRM record, or other request enters the workflow.

  2. 2
    Information gathered

    Relevant customer, product, pricing, document, and system context is assembled.

  3. 3
    Requirements interpreted

    AI can extract, compare, summarize, or prepare the information needed for the next step.

  4. 4
    Quote or proposal prepared

    Rules, calculations, templates, and approved content shape the draft.

  5. 5
    Human review

    The right person reviews material decisions, exceptions, and final output.

What can be included

  • Email, form, document, or CRM intake
  • Requirement extraction and missing-information checks
  • Product, service, supplier, or pricing lookups
  • Calculations and deterministic business rules
  • Proposal or quote document generation
  • CRM or quoting-system updates
  • Approval and exception routing
  • Notifications, tasks, and status tracking

What a finished implementation looks like

A working production workflow with the agreed integrations, controls, acceptance criteria, testing, documentation, handoff, and a bounded stabilization period. The exact solution depends on the systems, data, rules, and decisions involved in your quoting process.
