What Managed AI may include

Active monitoring

More active oversight of the supported solution and the operating signals that matter to its reliability.

More complex integration support

Recurring optimization

Continue improving AI behavior, workflow performance, usage, cost, and reliability where useful.

Ongoing improvements

What greater responsibility means

Managed AI means Palomma is more involved after launch than under AI Care or AI Optimize. The exact operating responsibility is defined for the specific solution rather than assumed from the package name.

What Managed AI does not promise

Managed AI does not mean unlimited support, unlimited changes, unlimited usage, or undefined response times. Those operating limits are agreed based on the actual solution and support burden.
