Common questions

Is this just dashboard design?

No. A dashboard is useful only if the underlying information is reliable and the measures actually help people make decisions. The work may include defining what should be measured, bringing information together, improving data quality, and then presenting it clearly.

Can you work with reports we already have?

Yes. We can improve existing reports when the main problem is that they are difficult to maintain, hard to understand, incomplete, or built from inconsistent information.

Can AI analyze our business data?

Sometimes. AI can help summarize or explain patterns, but it should not be used to hide unreliable source data or unclear business definitions. The information has to be trustworthy enough for the analysis being performed.
