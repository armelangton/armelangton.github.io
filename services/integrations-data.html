Integrations & Data

Connect your systems, improve how information moves between them, and make the data your business relies on more useful and reliable.

Possibilities

⇄

Connect business systems

Move customer, order, finance, or operational information between the tools that need it.

▦

Clean up important information

Fix inconsistent names, fields, formats, and records that make information harder to use.

!

Catch missing or failed updates

Find information that did not transfer correctly before it causes more work.

Good fit when

  • People copy the same information from one system to another
  • Different systems show different information about the same customer, order, or product
  • Important records are incomplete, duplicated, or inconsistent
  • People have to search several systems to find what they need
  • Disconnected systems cause delays or duplicate work

Integrations & Data may not be the right fit when

  • You know something is not working, but you do not know where the problem is
  • The systems already share information, but people still repeat the same manual steps
  • One system already has the right information, but the way people use that system is the problem

Common questions

Do we need to replace the systems we already use?

No. This work is often about getting the systems you already have to share information more effectively.

Can every system be connected?

No. Some software allows information to move in and out easily, while other software limits what can be accessed or shared.

Can you help clean up the information we already have?

Yes. We can fix duplicates, inconsistent fields, missing information, and other problems that make records difficult to use.
