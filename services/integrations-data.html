Integrations & Data

Connect the systems and information your business already depends on so people, workflows, applications, and AI can work from a more complete and reliable picture. We focus on the information that needs to move, where it should live, and what should happen next.

How This Work Is Scoped

Scope depends on the systems involved, data quality, synchronization needs, security, business rules, and how critical the connection is to daily operations.

What This Can Include

⇄

System Integrations

Connect CRM, ERP, accounting, email, forms, project tools, databases, and other business systems so information can move without unnecessary re-entry.

▦

Data Mapping and Transformation

Define how fields, records, formats, and business concepts translate between systems so the right information reaches the right place.

✓

Data Quality and Validation

Identify missing, inconsistent, duplicate, or unreliable information and establish practical rules for keeping important data usable.

↔

Synchronization and Handoffs

Keep agreed information aligned across systems and define when updates should flow one way, both ways, or only after review.

!

Exceptions and Failure Handling

Make failed syncs, missing information, conflicts, and unusual records visible instead of allowing silent errors to accumulate.

✦

AI-Ready Information

Prepare approved business information so AI can retrieve, compare, summarize, classify, or act on it with better context.

◎

Access and Governance

Define who and what can access information, which system is authoritative, and where human approval is required.

▤

Documentation and Testing

Document mappings, rules, dependencies, fallback paths, and test scenarios so the connection can be understood and maintained.

What Changes

  • People spend less time copying information between systems
  • Important data is easier to find and use
  • Handoffs between tools become more consistent
  • Data problems and integration failures become visible sooner
  • Automation and AI have better information to work with
  • Existing systems can work together without unnecessary replacement
