Integrations & Data

Connect the systems and information your business already depends on so people, workflows, applications, and AI can work from a more complete and reliable picture. We focus on what information needs to move, where it should live, which system is authoritative, and what should happen when something does not match.

How this service is scoped

Scope depends on the systems involved, data quality, authentication and API constraints, one-way or two-way synchronization, transformation rules, failure handling, and how critical the connection is to daily operations.

When this is a good fit

  • Employees copy information between systems because the tools do not talk to each other
  • Important information lives across email, spreadsheets, CRM, ERP, accounting, documents, or other systems
  • Different systems disagree about the same customer, order, supplier, product, or status
  • Automation or AI is limited because the underlying data is incomplete, inconsistent, or hard to access
  • Manual reconciliation is required before people can make decisions
  • You need to improve the flow of information without replacing useful core systems

Common questions

Do we need to replace our existing software?

No. Integration work is often specifically about getting more value from the systems you already have by improving how information moves between them.

Can every system be integrated?

No. Integration depends on what the software exposes, the quality of available APIs or exports, authentication requirements, vendor restrictions, and the reliability needed for the business process.

What if our data is messy?

That is common. The engagement can include mapping, cleanup rules, validation, deduplication, and defining the right source of truth before automation or AI depends on the data.

Why would an integration need ongoing service?

APIs, credentials, schemas, business rules, and third-party systems can change. Ongoing service is appropriate when Palomma remains responsible for monitoring and maintaining a business-critical connection.

Is important information stuck between systems?

Show us where people are copying, reconciling, or searching for data today. We will help identify the simplest reliable way to connect it.
