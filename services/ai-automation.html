Connected solution

AI & Automation

Connect AI assistance, workflow logic, business systems, and human review around a defined operational need.

Quick answer

This solution is for work that requires more than one assistant or a simple automation. Palomma designs a connected operating flow that can gather information, apply rules, use AI, update systems, request approval, and keep the right people informed.

Why this matters

The value comes from the complete workflow, not an isolated AI feature.

A useful solution often needs several capabilities working together. Information must enter correctly, context must be gathered, AI may prepare or recommend, systems must be updated, and people must review the decisions that carry risk. Designing that full chain is what turns a demo into dependable business support.

What the solution can combine

Intake and context

Capture the request, validate required information, and retrieve the records or documents needed to understand it.

AI analysis and drafting

Summarize, compare, classify, extract, recommend, or prepare a response using approved context and instructions.

Workflow and systems

Route work, update records, create tasks, generate documents, notify owners, and move the process forward.

Human control

Require review, approval, revision, escalation, or exception handling where business judgment remains essential.

Common connected solutions

  • Request intake, research, recommendation, approval, and follow-through
  • Account preparation, CRM updates, next-step guidance, and manager review
  • Document collection, extraction, comparison, exception detection, and routing
  • Proposal or quote preparation with rules, calculations, and human approval
  • Customer onboarding with information gathering, task coordination, and status visibility
  • Internal knowledge assistance connected to actions and operating workflows
  • Renewal or account review preparation across CRM, documents, and activity history

Common questions

How is this different from an AI assistant?

An assistant has one focused responsibility. A custom AI and automation solution coordinates multiple steps, systems, rules, and review points around a broader operational outcome.

Can this work with our current software?

Yes, when the systems provide appropriate access and the integration is technically and operationally responsible. The design identifies sources of truth and avoids creating unnecessary duplication.

How are security and permissions handled?

Access is limited according to the use case and available platform controls. Sensitive actions, data, and decisions are separated, logged, or routed for approval as required.

Can the solution be built in phases?

Yes. A phased approach can begin with the highest-value workflow and add integrations, automation, or AI capabilities after the foundation is proven.
