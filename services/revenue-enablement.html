Performance system

Revenue Enablement

Equip customer-facing teams with the training, guidance, content, tools, and operating support they need to perform with greater confidence and consistency.

Quick answer

Revenue enablement connects what people need to know with the work they are expected to complete. Palomma designs onboarding, playbooks, coaching, knowledge, messaging, and embedded guidance so teams can find and apply the right support at the right moment.

Why this matters

Good enablement is better operational design, not more content.

Teams rarely struggle because no information exists. They struggle because resources are scattered, expectations are unclear, training is disconnected from daily work, and managers cannot see where support is needed. Effective enablement makes the right behavior easier to understand and repeat.

What we design

Onboarding and ramp

Role-based learning paths, practice, milestones, manager checkpoints, and the resources new team members need to become productive.

Playbooks and guidance

Practical support for discovery, messaging, qualification, objections, follow-through, customer conversations, and internal processes.

Knowledge and content systems

Organized, searchable resources with ownership, naming, freshness, and clear placement inside the work.

Coaching and performance support

Observation, roleplay, feedback, reinforcement, scorecards, and guidance tied to real behaviors and outcomes.

Common enablement needs

  • New-hire onboarding and faster time to productive activity
  • Sales process, qualification, discovery, and opportunity guidance
  • Messaging by audience, industry, use case, or customer stage
  • Objection handling, roleplay, call preparation, and coaching resources
  • Product, service, process, and compliance knowledge
  • CRM adoption and workflow guidance inside daily execution
  • Renewal, expansion, account management, and customer-transition support

How the enablement system is built

  1. 1
    Define the performance need

    Identify the role, workflow, behavior, audience, business outcome, and evidence of the current gap.

  2. 2
    Map the moments of need

    Determine what people need before, during, and after the work, and where support should appear.

  3. 3
    Build the learning and guidance

    Create the training, practice, playbooks, examples, resources, coaching, and embedded support.

  4. 4
    Connect it to the workflow

    Place guidance in the CRM, knowledge system, task flow, manager routine, or application where it can be used.

  5. 5
    Measure and improve

    Track adoption, confidence, behavior, speed, quality, and business outcomes to guide ongoing refinement.

Common questions

Can you improve materials we already have?

Yes. Existing training, scripts, playbooks, process documents, recordings, and resources can be assessed, reorganized, simplified, and connected into a more usable system.

Is enablement only for sales teams?

No. The same approach can support account management, renewals, onboarding, customer-facing operations, managers, and other roles that need consistent execution.

Can AI support enablement?

Yes. AI can help employees find answers, practice conversations, prepare for work, summarize context, receive guidance, and use approved content more effectively.

How is enablement measured?

Measurement should connect participation and usage to confidence, observed behavior, workflow adoption, speed, quality, conversion, retention, or another relevant business outcome.
