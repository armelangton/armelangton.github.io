When Custom AI fits

Multiple workflows

The solution needs to connect or improve several related processes instead of one contained workflow.

Several systems or data sources

Important information must come together from multiple applications, documents, databases, email, or other business sources.

Substantial business-specific logic

The work depends on company-specific rules, exceptions, permissions, decisions, or context.

What it may include

Custom workspace or application

A dedicated interface may be appropriate when existing software does not adequately support the work.

Several teams or roles

Different people may need different views, responsibilities, approvals, or handoffs within the same solution.

Connected AI capabilities

Several AI-enabled functions may work together to understand information, retrieve context, reconcile, compare, validate, and prepare action.

AI stays central

Custom development is used to support the AI-enabled business result. This offer is not positioned as generic software development.
