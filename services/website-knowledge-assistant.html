Website Knowledge Assistant

Give website visitors useful answers grounded in the business information you choose to approve.

What it does

The Website Knowledge Assistant is designed to answer questions from an approved business knowledge set instead of relying on a general-purpose chatbot to improvise. It retrieves relevant approved information, uses that evidence to prepare an answer, and can decline when the available knowledge does not support a reliable response.

What knowledge it can use

Website content

Approved pages or website material that should be available to the assistant.

Files

Approved documents that contain information visitors may need to find or understand.

Business text

Approved policies, explanations, service details, instructions, or other maintained knowledge.

Questions and answers

Approved Q&A content for information that needs a clear, controlled response.

How answers stay grounded

  1. 1
    A visitor asks a question

    The assistant receives the question within the website experience.

  2. 2
    Relevant approved knowledge is retrieved

    The system searches the tenant's approved knowledge rather than treating the open web as the source of truth.

  3. 3
    The answer is generated from evidence

    The model is instructed to use the retrieved evidence and stay within the supported business knowledge boundary.

  4. 4
    Unsupported questions are bounded

    If the available evidence is not strong enough, the assistant can return an unsupported outcome instead of pretending to know.

Where it fits

The assistant can support visitors who are trying to understand services, policies, processes, product information, requirements, documentation, or other approved business knowledge without manually searching through multiple pages or files.

Current capabilities

  • Approved website, file, text, and Q&A knowledge sources
  • Tenant-scoped knowledge boundaries
  • Grounded retrieval before answer generation
  • Relevance thresholds for unsupported questions
  • Citation-ready retrieved evidence
  • Structured model responses within a defined answer contract
  • Branded tenant context for the website experience

Current boundaries

This is not being presented as a self-service SaaS platform. Customer self-service knowledge management, persistent customer administration, and lead-delivery workflows are separate capabilities that should only be added and marketed when they are fully operationalized.
