When a Quick Win fits

One focused job

A specific task is taking too much research, interpretation, checking, comparison, or repetitive effort.

Contained scope

The problem can be solved without redesigning a full end-to-end process or connecting several major workflows.

Useful AI work

AI needs to understand, compare, validate, reconcile, or prepare information—not simply add another chatbot.

Examples of Quick Wins

Commercial Comparison

Bring relevant supplier, product, pricing, availability, historical, and system information together so people can compare viable commercial choices.

AI Order Processing

Understand, validate, and prepare incoming customer orders from email, purchase orders, spreadsheets, or documents.

Supplier Order Review

Compare supplier acknowledgments or updates with what the business expected and surface meaningful differences.

These are examples, not the entire catalog. A different focused business problem may also fit a Quick Win.

When the scope becomes larger

  • If several steps, people, approvals, or systems must work together, AI Workflow is usually the better fit.
  • If several workflows, teams, systems, or substantial custom logic are involved, Custom AI Solution is usually the better fit.
