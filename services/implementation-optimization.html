Common questions

Can you work on something another provider built?

Potentially, yes. We first review what exists, what is working, what is incomplete or unreliable, and whether improving the current solution is the best use of effort.

Do we have to start over?

No. Starting over is not automatically the answer. We look for the smallest change that will reliably solve the problem, which may mean finishing, simplifying, reconfiguring, or replacing only part of what exists.

Can this include training or changes to how people use the system?

Yes. Sometimes the technology is not the only issue. Clearer workflows, better guidance, role changes, or training may be needed so the system and the people using it work together properly.
