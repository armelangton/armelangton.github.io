When AI Workflow fits

Several connected steps

The work moves through a repeatable process rather than one isolated task.

People and systems interact

Information moves between employees, email, CRM, ERP, accounting, documents, or other tools.

Interpretation leads to action

AI needs to understand information and then help route, prepare, validate, approve, or trigger the next step.

What a workflow can include

Intake and context

Read incoming information and retrieve the relevant customer, supplier, product, historical, or business context.

Rules, exceptions, and approvals

Apply business rules, surface missing or inconsistent information, and preserve human approval where it matters.

Connected next steps

Prepare or move work into existing systems, create follow-on actions, or route the work to the right person.

Examples

Potential workflows include purchasing, supplier processes, customer orders, RFQ-to-quote work, service workflows, and other multi-step business processes.

When it becomes Custom AI

If several workflows, teams, systems, or major data sources are involved—or the work requires substantial company-specific logic or a custom application—Custom AI Solution is usually the better fit.
