What AI Care may include

Monitoring

Appropriate monitoring to help identify obvious operating issues or failures in the supported solution.

Troubleshooting

Investigate and correct problems when the delivered AI solution or connected workflow stops behaving as intended.

Integration and provider changes

Address appropriate API, integration, model-provider, or connected-service changes within the agreed support scope.

Business-rule updates

Adjust agreed rules or configuration when the way the business operates changes.

Knowledge or source updates

Maintain agreed knowledge sources or information inputs where the supported solution depends on them.

What AI Care does not mean

AI Care is not unlimited support, unlimited changes, unlimited usage, or an open-ended development retainer. The supported responsibilities are defined for the specific solution.

When to choose something more involved

If you want regular tuning and proactive improvements, AI Optimize is the better fit. If you want Palomma to take greater ongoing operating responsibility, Managed AI is the better fit.

Want help after launch?

We can define the right level of ongoing involvement based on what was built and how important it is to your operations.
