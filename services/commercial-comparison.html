The Business Problem

↓

Information is scattered

Supplier emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, product information, pricing, availability, lead times, and system records may all matter to one decision.

⇄

Context has to be reconciled

Employees often have to determine which information belongs together and which source is current or relevant.

!

Missing information matters

Important gaps, conflicts, and exceptions can change the commercial decision.

✓

The output must be useful

The goal is not a summary. It is a clearer set of viable choices and the context needed to decide.

How the Work Can Flow

  1. 1

    Gather

    Bring together the relevant supplier, product, pricing, availability, historical, and system information.

  2. 2

    Understand

    Interpret unstructured information from email, PDFs, spreadsheets, and documents.

  3. 3

    Compare

    Reconcile alternatives using business context, rules, history, and meaningful differences.

  4. 4

    Prepare

    Present viable options, missing information, and exceptions for the next decision.

Good Fit When

  • People gather information from several places before making a commercial decision
  • Supplier, product, pricing, or availability information frequently changes
  • Historical transactions or business rules affect the right choice
  • Your existing CRM, ERP, or accounting system does not eliminate the research around the decision
