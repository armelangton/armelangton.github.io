Custom system

Business Applications

Replace disconnected spreadsheets, inboxes, and workarounds with a practical application built around the way your business actually operates.

Quick answer

A business application gives your team one connected place to manage a process that existing tools do not support well. It can combine records, workflow, reporting, documents, approvals, and AI assistance without forcing the operation into a generic product.

Why this matters

Stop making the team hold the system together.

When a process lives across spreadsheets, email, shared folders, forms, and people’s memory, the team becomes the integration layer. A purpose-built application brings the records and workflow together, makes status visible, and creates a stable foundation for automation and AI.

What we build

Operational workspaces

Manage requests, accounts, projects, cases, suppliers, engagements, or other business records in one place.

Guided workflows

Lead users through the right steps, required information, approvals, handoffs, and next actions.

Decision support

Bring together history, context, calculations, comparisons, and AI recommendations where decisions happen.

Management visibility

Show status, ownership, exceptions, workload, performance, and the information leaders need to act.

When a custom application makes sense

  • The process is important but does not fit a standard product
  • Employees copy the same information across multiple tools
  • Spreadsheets have become the operational system of record
  • Handoffs, approvals, or status are difficult to see
  • The business needs calculations, rules, or context unique to its operation
  • Existing software is too complex, too expensive, or missing critical workflow
  • AI needs a reliable interface, data structure, and human review process

How the application is designed

  1. 1
    Define the operation

    Clarify the users, records, workflow, decisions, reporting, pain points, and business outcome.

  2. 2
    Design the experience

    Map the information architecture, screens, permissions, states, actions, and handoffs before full development.

  3. 3
    Build the working system

    Create the database, interface, workflow, integrations, and agreed AI or automation capabilities.

  4. 4
    Validate with real scenarios

    Test data, permissions, normal paths, exceptions, usability, mobile needs, and reporting.

  5. 5
    Launch and transfer ownership

    Prepare data, train users, document administration, and stabilize the application after launch.

Common questions

Is this the same as buying software?

No. A custom application is designed around a defined business process. It is most useful when standard software creates excessive workarounds or cannot support the operation without major compromise.

Can the application connect to our existing systems?

Yes, where appropriate APIs, permissions, data quality, and security requirements allow it. The scope identifies which system should remain the source of truth.

Will our team be dependent on Palomma?

The project includes documentation, training, and handover. Ongoing hosting and management can be provided, but the solution is designed so the team understands how it works and what it owns.

Can we begin with a smaller first version?

Yes. A focused first release around the most valuable workflow is often the best way to reduce risk, learn from users, and add capability in deliberate stages.
