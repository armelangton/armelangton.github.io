Common questions

Do we need custom software if we already have a CRM or ERP?

Not necessarily. We use what already works and build only when a focused application is the simpler answer.

Can it connect to our current tools?

Yes, when those systems provide a reliable way to connect.

Does every application need AI?

No. AI is included only when it improves the work.
