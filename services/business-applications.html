Common questions

How do I know if we actually need a custom application?

A custom application becomes worth considering when the work has a real business need that existing software cannot support without substantial workarounds, disconnected tools, or repeated manual effort. We do not recommend building new software when improving what you already have would solve the problem.

Does a business application replace our other systems?

Not necessarily. It can fill a gap between existing systems, provide a better workspace for a specific job, or bring information and actions from several tools into one place while the underlying systems continue doing what they already do well.

Can it connect to our existing software?

Often, yes. The application can exchange information with existing systems when those systems provide a reliable way to access and update the information needed.

Does a business application have to include AI?

No. AI, automation, integrations, and reporting are added only when they improve the work the application is being built to support.
