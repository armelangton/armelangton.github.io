Business Applications

When spreadsheets, inboxes, shared drives, or off-the-shelf software no longer fit the work, Palomma can build a focused business application around the process you actually need. The goal is not software for its own sake; it is a clearer, easier way to manage important work.

How this service is scoped

Scope depends on the process being supported, number of users and roles, data and integrations involved, custom logic, reporting needs, and whether AI capabilities are part of the application.

When this is a good fit

  • Your team is running an important process through spreadsheets, email, shared folders, or manual trackers
  • Existing software has too many workarounds or does not match the way the business operates
  • People cannot easily see status, ownership, history, exceptions, or the next step
  • You need one practical workspace that brings information and actions together
  • Different roles need different views or responsibilities
  • You want AI built into a useful business tool rather than added as a separate chatbot

What affects scope

  • Number of users, roles, and views

Common questions

Do we need a custom application if we already have CRM or ERP software?

Not necessarily. Palomma should use what already works. A custom application makes sense when important work still happens outside those systems, when the current interface creates too much friction, or when several systems need a simpler operational layer around them.

Can the application connect to our current tools?

Yes, when the systems provide reliable ways to connect. Integrations may include CRM, ERP, accounting, email, documents, forms, calendars, databases, or other business systems.

Can AI be part of the application?

Yes. AI can be embedded where it helps users understand information, compare options, prepare work, search knowledge, or make recommendations. It does not need to be the entire application.

Will this become a huge software project?

It should not by default. We scope the smallest useful application around the business problem and expand only when the additional capability is justified.

Working around software instead of with it?

Show us the process and the workarounds. We will help determine whether configuration, integration, automation, or a focused application is the better answer.
