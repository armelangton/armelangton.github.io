Good fit when

  • Important work is spread across spreadsheets, inboxes, and other tools
  • Existing software forces people to work around it
  • Your team needs one place to see information, status, and next steps
  • A specialized process does not fit standard software
  • Your current tools cannot provide the functionality the work requires

A custom application may not be the right fit when

  • Existing software already handles the work well
  • The work follows clear, repeatable steps — workflow automation may be a better option
  • You know something isn’t working, but you don’t know what needs to change — start with an assessment
  • The main problem is that your systems do not share information — integration work may be a better option

Common questions

How do I know if we actually need custom software?

Usually when important work no longer fits the tools you have and the workarounds are costing time, creating delays, or making the work harder.

Can a business application work with the systems we already have?

Yes, if those systems allow the application to exchange the information it needs.

Does a custom application have to include AI?

No. AI is included only when it makes the application more useful.
