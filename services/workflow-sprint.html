Common questions

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. Most workflow projects improve how your existing systems work together.

Can AI be part of the workflow?

Yes. AI can handle steps such as reading, classifying, comparing, drafting, or recommending when useful.

Can people still approve important steps?

Yes. Human approval can remain anywhere judgment or authority is required.
