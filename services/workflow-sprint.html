Good fit when

  • Work is repetitive
  • People copy or re-enter information
  • Handoffs slow things down
  • Routine follow-up gets missed
  • People spend time moving work between systems
  • The process is clear but too manual

Automation may not be the right fit when

  • The process needs to be fixed first
  • The work changes completely every time
  • Human judgment drives most of the work
  • The problem itself is still unclear
  • Required systems cannot reliably connect
  • Automating it would add complexity instead of removing it

Common questions

Do we have to replace the software we already use?

No. Automation often connects and improves the tools you already have.

What kinds of work can be automated?

Repeatable work such as intake, routing, updates, approvals, notifications, handoffs, and data movement is often a good place to start.

Can AI be part of an automated workflow?

Yes. AI can handle parts that require reading, understanding, comparing, or creating while automation moves the work through the process.
