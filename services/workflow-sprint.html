Workflow Automation

Improve a repeatable business process from one end to the other. Palomma connects the steps, systems, information, handoffs, approvals, and exceptions involved so work moves forward with less copying, chasing, checking, and manual coordination.

How this service is scoped

Scope depends on how many steps and systems are involved, where decisions and approvals occur, how complex the exceptions are, and whether Palomma continues to operate or maintain the workflow after launch.

When this is a good fit

  • Employees re-enter the same information in multiple places
  • Work gets delayed because people are waiting on emails, reminders, approvals, or status checks
  • A process crosses several systems and no one has a clear view of what happens next
  • Incomplete requests or missing information create repeated back-and-forth
  • Manual handoffs are causing errors, missed steps, or inconsistent follow-through
  • You want AI inside a process without turning the whole process over to AI

What Palomma can automate

Automation can be simple or highly connected. The goal is not to automate every step; it is to remove unnecessary manual effort while preserving the decisions people still need to make.

Common questions

How is workflow automation different from an AI agent?

A workflow coordinates the full process: steps, systems, people, approvals, and exceptions. An AI agent may perform one or more jobs inside that process, but the workflow is the larger operating flow.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

No. Many workflow projects are specifically designed to improve the work around existing CRM, ERP, accounting, email, document, or project systems.

Can people still approve important steps?

Yes. Human approvals can be built into any point where judgment, authority, risk, or accountability makes them appropriate.

What happens when an automated step fails?

Reliable workflows need exception handling. We define what gets retried, what is logged, who is notified, and how the work continues manually when needed.

Have a process that takes too much coordination?

Show us how the work moves today. We will help identify what should be simplified, connected, automated, or left in human hands.
