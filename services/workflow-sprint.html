FAQ

Can you automate tools we already use?

Usually, yes. We first confirm what each system supports and how the information should move.

Can approvals stay with our team?

Yes. Automation can pause for a person whenever review or approval is required.

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. The goal is often to make the systems you already use work together better.
