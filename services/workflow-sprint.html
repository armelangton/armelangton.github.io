Good fit when

  • The same steps are repeated again and again
  • People copy or re-enter the same information
  • Work slows down waiting for handoffs or approvals
  • Routine follow-up gets missed
  • People spend too much time moving work between systems

Automation may not be the right fit when

  • You know something isn’t working, but you don’t know what needs to change
  • The work changes based on information that has to be understood or compared
  • The systems cannot share the information the workflow needs

Common questions

Do we have to replace the software we already use?

No. Automation often connects and improves the tools you already have.

What kinds of work can be automated?

Repeatable work such as intake, routing, updates, approvals, notifications, handoffs, and moving information between systems.

Can AI be part of an automated workflow?

Yes. AI can handle parts that involve reading, understanding, comparing, or creating while automation moves the work through the process.
