Workflow Automation

Automate repeatable work so information, tasks, approvals, updates, and next steps move forward without someone having to manage each one by hand.

Starting at $2,500+

Possibilities

Capture and route incoming work

Take requests, forms, orders, leads, documents, or other incoming work and automatically send the right information to the right place or person.

Move work through the next steps

Create tasks, request approvals, send reminders, update status, and trigger the next step when a known condition is met.

Keep systems and people in sync

Move information between the tools you use and update records automatically so people do not have to copy, re-enter, or chase the same information.

Good fit when

  • People repeat the same administrative steps for every request, order, lead, project, or transaction
  • Information is copied, re-entered, or manually moved from one system to another
  • Work regularly waits for someone to assign it, route it, update it, or tell another person what happens next
  • Approvals, reminders, notifications, or routine follow-up depend on someone remembering to send them
  • The business already knows what should happen next in most situations, but people are still doing those steps manually

Another solution may be a better fit when

  • The work cannot follow predictable rules because someone has to understand, compare, or interpret changing information — AI Agents & Assistants may be a better option
  • People need a new place to manage records, work, or decisions — a Business Application may be a better option
  • The current process has unnecessary steps, unclear responsibilities, or other problems and you are not yet sure what should be automated — Systems & Process Improvement may be a better place to start
