Workflow Automation

Connect the steps, systems, information, and approvals around repeatable work so your team can move faster with fewer manual handoffs.

Quick answer

Workflow automation improves one defined process from beginning to end. Palomma maps the current workflow, removes unnecessary steps, connects the right tools, and automates routine movement while preserving human decisions and exceptions.

Why this matters

Revenue slows down when the work between systems is invisible.

Requests wait in inboxes. Records are updated twice. Approvals depend on someone remembering. Teams cannot see what is complete, blocked, or missing. A well-designed workflow makes ownership and next steps clear, then automates the routine movement around them.

What we can automate

Intake and routing

Capture requests, validate required information, assign ownership, and direct work to the right path.

Handoffs and approvals

Move work between teams, request review, document decisions, and escalate delays without relying on memory.

System updates

Keep CRM, forms, spreadsheets, project tools, inboxes, and shared records aligned where appropriate.

Notifications and follow-through

Trigger reminders, tasks, status changes, documents, and next actions based on defined events.

Common questions

Do we need to replace our current tools?

Usually not. The first goal is to improve how the tools you already use work together. Replacement is recommended only when the current system creates a structural limitation that configuration or integration cannot solve.

Can the workflow include human approvals?

Yes. Human judgment and approval can be built directly into the process, with the surrounding preparation, routing, reminders, and recordkeeping automated.

What happens when an automation fails?

The design includes error visibility, ownership, recovery steps, and manual alternatives appropriate to the importance of the workflow.

Can we automate only part of a process?

Yes. A focused improvement around the highest-friction steps can be more valuable and easier to adopt than automating everything at once.
