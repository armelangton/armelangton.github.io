Workflow Automation

Improve a repeatable business process by connecting the steps, information, systems, decisions, approvals, and people involved. Automation can be simple or AI-enabled; the goal is to remove unnecessary manual coordination without losing the controls the business needs.

How This Work Is Scoped

Scope depends on process breadth, number and complexity of systems, information sources, decision rules, exception paths, and whether AI is needed inside the workflow.

What This Can Include

↓

Intake and Validation

Capture the right information up front and identify incomplete, inconsistent, or unusable requests before they create downstream work.

↗

Routing and Ownership

Use business rules to assign responsibility, direct work to the correct path, and make the next owner clear.

✓

Approvals and Human Decisions

Keep required judgment and authorization in the process with clear review points, records, and escalation paths.

⇄

System Connections

Move information between CRM, ERP, email, forms, spreadsheets, project tools, databases, and other business systems.

✦

AI Inside the Workflow

Use AI for extraction, classification, comparison, summarization, drafting, or recommendation where it improves the process.

!

Exception Handling

Make unusual requests, missing information, delays, and failures visible with clear manual or alternate paths.

▤

Process Visibility

Create useful status, history, notifications, and reporting so people can see where work is and what needs attention.

✓

Testing and Documentation

Test representative scenarios and document the workflow, ownership, rules, dependencies, and recovery paths.

What Changes

  • Work enters the process with better information
  • Ownership and next steps become clearer
  • Routine movement between systems requires less manual effort
  • Approvals and handoffs become more consistent
  • Exceptions become visible sooner
  • People spend less time copying, reminding, and checking status
