Common questions

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. Most projects improve how your existing systems work together.

Can AI be part of the workflow?

Yes, when reading, classifying, comparing, drafting, or recommending helps.

Can people still approve important steps?

Yes. Human approval can stay anywhere judgment or authority is required.
