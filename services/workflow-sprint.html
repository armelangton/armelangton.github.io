Connect the steps that already have a clear path.

Workflow automation handles the repeatable coordination around a process: capturing incoming work, creating or updating records, routing information, requesting approvals, sending reminders, changing status, and triggering the next step when a known condition is met.

It can connect the systems you already use—such as CRM, email, forms, accounting, project tools, or other business applications—so information moves without people copying it from place to place. Human approval can stay in the workflow wherever judgment still belongs with a person.
