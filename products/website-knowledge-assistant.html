AI Product

Website Knowledge Assistant

Give website visitors useful answers grounded in the business information you choose to approve.

Useful answers without an open-ended chatbot

The Website Knowledge Assistant is built around an approved business knowledge set. When a visitor asks a question, it retrieves relevant approved information first, uses that evidence to prepare the answer, and can decline when the available knowledge does not support a reliable response.

The knowledge stays under your control

Website content

Approved pages and website material that should be available to the assistant.

Files

Approved documents containing information visitors may need to find or understand.

Business knowledge

Approved policies, explanations, service details, instructions, and maintained text.

Questions & answers

Approved Q&A content for information that benefits from a clear, controlled response.

How an answer is produced

  1. 1
    A visitor asks a question

    The question enters the website assistant experience.

  2. 2
    Relevant approved knowledge is retrieved

    The system searches the approved knowledge assigned to that business rather than treating the open web as the source of truth.

  3. 3
    The answer is built from evidence

    The model is instructed to stay within the retrieved business knowledge and defined answer boundary.

  4. 4
    Unsupported questions stay bounded

    If the evidence is not strong enough, the assistant can return an unsupported outcome instead of pretending to know.

Where it fits

The assistant can help visitors understand services, policies, processes, product information, requirements, documentation, and other approved business knowledge without making them search through multiple pages or files.

What the product supports today

  • Approved website, file, text, and Q&A knowledge sources
  • Tenant-scoped knowledge boundaries
  • Grounded retrieval before answer generation
  • Relevance thresholds for unsupported questions
  • Citation-ready retrieved evidence
  • Structured model responses within a defined answer contract
  • Branded tenant context for the website experience

This is not being presented as a self-service SaaS platform. Customer self-service knowledge management, persistent customer administration, and lead-delivery workflows remain separate capabilities and should only be marketed when they are operational.
