A scalable training system for a growing remote sales team.

The challenge

Training had become too important to manage informally.

The sales team needed to learn increasingly complex material across messaging, discovery, qualification, objection handling, CRM expectations, cybersecurity, compliance, and service knowledge.

The content existed, but it was spread across documents and resources rather than delivered through a consistent learning experience. With no dedicated sales trainer supporting the distributed team, the business needed a way to make learning more repeatable and less dependent on live instruction.

The approach

Turn training resources into a structured learning experience.

The material was reorganized around specific skills and learning objectives instead of simply being stored as reference documents. The system introduced courses, modules, flash cards, knowledge checks, scenario-based practice, sequenced learning programs, progress tracking, resume functionality, and search.

Structured learning

Courses and programs break complex topics into manageable sequences rather than leaving learners to navigate a document library.

Practice and reinforcement

Flash cards, knowledge checks, and scenarios reinforce understanding without requiring another live training session.

Continuity

Progress, Save & exit, and resume state give remote learners a reliable way to stop and return without losing their place.

Findability

Search and My Learning make it easier to revisit material after the initial onboarding experience.

Administration

Learning management, not a static content library.

The Learning Workspace includes learner assignments, published and changed course states, browser-local drafts, learner preview, progress reporting, and per-course completion visibility.

The public demo intentionally stays bounded to what was implemented. It does not simulate enterprise user management, certificates, due dates, or organization-wide backend reporting.

The outcome

A more consistent and repeatable way to train a distributed team.

The Learning Workspace turned a growing body of training material into a central place for structured onboarding, ongoing learning, practice, and reference while reducing dependence on one-time instruction.

Broader onboarding result

The larger onboarding and enablement transformation shortened a process that had historically taken roughly a month to about a week. That result reflects the broader onboarding initiative rather than the LMS alone.

What this demonstrates

Turning an operational scaling problem into a working system.

The project connects process design, onboarding, training operations, knowledge management, sales operations, information architecture, workflow design, user experience, and implementation.
