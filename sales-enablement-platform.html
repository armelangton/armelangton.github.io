Selected Work

Sales Enablement Platform

A sales workspace that brings seller priorities, customer context, resources, coaching, and AI guidance into the work itself so reps can understand what needs attention and act without jumping between systems.

The problem

Sales reps often work across the CRM, notes, training, playbooks, shared files, calendars, and separate AI tools. The information may exist, but it is disconnected from the work that needs to happen next.

How the system works

The workspace connects the seller’s work with the context and guidance needed to complete it.

1 · PrioritizeSurface the accounts, opportunities, and tasks that need attention.
2 · UnderstandBring customer history, notes, activity, and opportunity context into view.
3 · AssistUse AI to summarize context, suggest next actions, and surface relevant coaching or resources.
4 · ActMove from guidance into calls, tasks, notes, and opportunity work without losing context.

What it does

Prioritized work

Turns open activity into a clearer daily view of what deserves attention first.

Account and opportunity context

Keeps customer information, recent activity, notes, and deal details close to the work.

AI guidance

Helps interpret account context, recommend a next step, prepare for conversations, and answer questions without forcing the rep into a separate AI tool.

Embedded enablement

Surfaces playbooks, learning, coaching, and supporting resources where they are useful instead of making reps search for them later.

Why it matters

The value is not just consolidating information. The workspace helps turn scattered sales data and resources into a clearer decision about what to do next, while keeping the rep in control of the action.

Palomma’s role

Designed the operating workflow and product experience around the seller’s actual work rather than treating the CRM, training, and AI as separate destinations.
