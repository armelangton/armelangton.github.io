Sales Enablement Platform

Challenge

Sales reps often have to jump between the CRM, training, playbooks, notes, shared files, and AI tools to find what they need for the work in front of them.

What we built

One sales workspace that brings the work and the information needed to complete it together.

  • Prioritized daily work and next steps
  • Customer and opportunity information
  • Calls, tasks, events, and notes
  • Playbooks, learning, coaching, and AI support where reps need them

Why it matters

Reps spend less time searching across systems and can get the right information and guidance while they are doing the work.
