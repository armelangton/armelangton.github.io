Fire Protection Field Assistant

A field workspace that connects job context, required checks, documentation, reference material, and AI-assisted guidance so technicians can move through the work without hunting across separate systems.

The problem

Field employees may need customer history, site details, previous work, inspection requirements, manuals, and internal resources before and during a job. When that information lives in different places, preparation slows down and technicians have to interrupt the work to find what they need.

How the system works

The workspace keeps the job, the required work, and the information needed to complete it in one operating flow.

1 · PrepareBring customer, site, equipment, and previous-job context into view before the technician starts.
2 · PerformGuide the technician through required checks, readings, notes, photos, and issues as the work happens.
3 · AssistUse AI-assisted guidance and connected reference material to help surface relevant procedures, manuals, and job context when questions come up.
4 · CompleteKeep documentation, unresolved issues, and follow-up work connected to the job instead of rebuilding the record afterward.

What it does

Job context

Keeps customer, location, equipment, and prior-work information close to the active field job.

Guided field work

Organizes required checks, readings, notes, photos, and issues around the work being performed.

AI-assisted guidance

Helps technicians find and interpret relevant job context and reference material without leaving the workflow to search across separate resources.

Connected documentation

Keeps evidence, completion details, and follow-up items tied to the job so the record is easier to carry forward.

Reference material in the workflow

Why it matters

The value is not simply putting field forms on a screen. The workspace connects preparation, execution, reference support, documentation, and follow-up so technicians can spend less time searching and keep more of the job context intact from start to finish.

Palomma’s role

Designed the field operating workflow and product experience around the technician’s actual job—from preparation and required checks through documentation, reference support, and completion.
