How to Read the Work

Problem first. Architecture second. Evidence always.

Business problem

What recurring work was difficult, fragmented, slow, or inconsistent?

What was implemented

What workflow, system, agent, integration, interface, or control was built around that problem?

How it was controlled

Where did rules, validation, permissions, exception handling, or human approval belong?

What can be measured

Where verified evidence exists, focus on turnaround, time per transaction, manual touches, rework, routing, backlog, or throughput rather than speculative ROI.
