After launch

Own the solution. Choose whether Palomma continues to manage it.

Fixed-fee implementations include the deployment, documentation, training, and correction period defined in the engagement. Managed service is available when you want Palomma to continue operating and maintaining the solution.

Client managed

Your team operates the solution using the accounts, documentation, and training included in the engagement. Future changes can be scoped as needed.

Palomma managed

Palomma continues providing the hosting, monitoring, maintenance, usage, adjustments, and support specifically listed in the monthly agreement.

If managed service ends, Palomma stops providing those ongoing services. Ownership, accounts, data, and transfer terms are defined before implementation.

How pricing works

Starting prices reflect a defined standard scope.

Your final fixed price is based on the work required to deliver the agreed outcome. Additional workflows, integrations, users, systems, data preparation, custom interfaces, security requirements, usage, or operating responsibility can change the scope.

You will receive the complete fixed implementation price and, when applicable, the fixed monthly price before work begins.
