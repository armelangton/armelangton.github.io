Where the Opportunity Starts

If your team keeps reading, checking, copying, routing, looking up, preparing, or re-entering the same information, the problem may be the workflow—not the people.

Many important business processes already run through email, documents, spreadsheets, CRMs, ERPs, forms, and other software—but still rely on people to move information between them manually. Palomma focuses on recurring work where AI, automation, connected systems, business rules, and human review can create a better operating workflow.

AI Agents & Implementation Capabilities

AI agents can do important work. They do not have to do all of it.

Palomma builds agents and agentic workflows where interpretation, retrieval, language, reasoning, or flexible action improves the work. They can operate alongside deterministic rules, integrations, existing software, and human approval rather than replacing the entire process.

Implementation Philosophy

Not every step should be AI.

AI is useful where work requires interpretation, language, retrieval, pattern recognition, or flexible reasoning. Deterministic rules are often better where consistency matters. Existing systems should keep doing jobs they already handle well. Human approval belongs where judgment, authority, risk, or consequence makes it appropriate.

How a Project Works

From workflow problem to working implementation.

1

Understand

2
