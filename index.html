Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. Start with what is slowing people down, creating extra work, or not working the way it should. We can help determine whether the answer is AI, automation, a better-connected system, new software, or changes to the way the work gets done.

Do you only work with AI?

No. AI is a specialty, not the answer to every problem. We also automate work, connect systems and data, improve existing systems and processes, and build business applications when those are a better fit.
