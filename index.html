Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which service is right for my business?

You do not need to identify the solution before contacting Palomma. Start with the business problem, where work is slowing down, or what your team needs to improve. We will help determine the right service, scope, and approach.

Can Palomma work with the systems we already use?

Yes. We use the tools and systems your business already relies on whenever practical. The goal is to improve how work gets done, not replace useful software without a reason.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the service, implementation scope, systems involved, complexity, and any ongoing operational responsibility. Starting prices are shown where they can be stated responsibly; final scope is confirmed before work begins.

What happens after launch?

That depends on the solution. Some work can be handed over after implementation, while other solutions include an ongoing operating component. We define that responsibility clearly before the engagement begins.

How do you keep AI-supported work under human control?

AI-supported work is built around approved information, defined rules, testing, review points, and exception paths. When judgment, approval, or business authority is required, a person remains responsible for the decision.
