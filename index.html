The workflow is often the problem

If your team keeps reading, checking, copying, routing, looking up, preparing, or re-entering the same information, the problem may be the workflow—not the people.

Palomma starts with the recurring operational responsibility and the friction around it. The goal is not to add AI everywhere. It is to make the work more reliable, connected, and easier to run.

Implementation philosophy

Not every step should be AI.

AI belongs where interpretation, language, judgment, or flexible reasoning helps. Deterministic rules, system controls, and human approval stay in the workflow where they are more reliable. Palomma uses the right combination for the responsibility being solved.
