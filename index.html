Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the fixed fee?

Each engagement has a defined scope, deliverables, assumptions, and support period. Your complete fixed fee is agreed before work begins so you know what is included and what would require additional scope.

How do I know which option fits my project?

Start with the business problem. The Opportunity Session is designed for one defined challenge, while the Assessment is for a broader sales process or system that needs deeper analysis. Implementation work is scoped around the workflow, systems, integrations, data, and outcomes involved.

What does “starting at” mean?

The published starting price applies to the standard scope described for that service. Additional workflows, integrations, data requirements, users, systems, or custom development may change the fixed fee. Any change is defined and agreed before work begins.

What can Palomma build?

Palomma focuses on sales systems and can combine process improvement, CRM, workflow automation, AI assistants, integrations, and custom business applications when those tools support the problem being solved.

How long does an implementation take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope, system access, data, integrations, testing, and coordination required. Your timeline is defined with the project scope before implementation begins.

Who owns the solution and the data?

You own your data, branding, business configuration, and outputs. Palomma retains ownership of its reusable framework, code, templates, deployment tools, and methods. Your agreement includes a licence to use the solution configured for your business.

What happens after launch?

Each implementation includes the support period stated in its scope. If ongoing support is useful after that, responsibilities and requirements are defined first and priced as a separate fixed-fee scope.

Will my team be dependent on Palomma?

No. The goal is to deliver a working solution your team can understand and use. Documentation and training are included where applicable so the solution does not depend on continuous consulting support.
