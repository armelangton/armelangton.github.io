About Palomma

Palomma builds practical AI-enabled solutions around the way a business actually works. The focus is not on adding AI everywhere. It is on improving a real workflow, connecting the information and systems it depends on, and using the right mix of AI, automation, rules, and human judgment.

Palomma is founder-led and hands-on. Work is scoped around a defined operational responsibility or problem, built with the systems and constraints of the business in mind, tested before launch, and handed over with clear ownership.

That approach can result in a custom AI solution, a connected workflow, or a focused AI product when a repeatable product is the better fit.
