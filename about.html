About Palomma

Practical AI-enabled operational implementation.

Palomma is a founder-led implementation company focused on improving real recurring workflows. The work starts with the operational responsibility—not with a technology menu—and uses the right combination of AI, automation, deterministic rules, integrations, custom software, and human judgment.

Projects are scoped around a defined production responsibility, built with the customer’s systems and constraints in mind, validated before launch, and handed over with clear ownership. Optional continuation can be scoped when additional maintenance, optimization, enhancement, or managed responsibility is actually needed.

AI should earn its place in the workflow. Where a rule, system control, or human decision is more dependable, that is what should be used.
