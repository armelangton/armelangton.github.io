About Palomma

Practical AI implementation built around real business work.

Palomma is a founder-led AI implementation company focused on recurring operational workflows. The work starts with what the business is trying to accomplish—not with a predetermined AI tool.

Why Palomma Exists

AI is useful when it improves how the work actually gets done.

Businesses now have access to powerful AI tools, but access alone does not integrate those tools into the workflows, systems, data, rules, and decisions that keep the business operating. Palomma focuses on that implementation gap.

Implementation Philosophy

AI should earn its place in the workflow.

Palomma uses AI where interpretation, retrieval, language, pattern recognition, or flexible reasoning adds value. Where a deterministic rule is more dependable, use the rule. Where an existing system already does the job well, use the system. Where judgment or authority matters, keep the human involved. The objective is not maximum AI. It is a better operating workflow.

Founder-Led Implementation

Direct involvement from problem definition through implementation.

Palomma is founder-led and hands-on. That means continuity from the first workflow conversation through scoping, implementation, validation, and handoff. The aim is to keep the business problem, operating constraints, and implementation responsibility connected instead of separating strategy from delivery.

Ownership & Handoff

Built to become part of your business—not a permanent consulting dependency.

Projects are scoped around a defined responsibility, implemented with the business's systems and constraints in mind, tested before launch, and handed over with clear documentation and ownership. Additional maintenance, enhancement, optimization, or managed responsibility can be scoped separately when it makes sense.
