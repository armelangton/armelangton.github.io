About Palomma

Practical AI-enabled business improvement.

Palomma is founder-led and hands-on. The work starts with a real workflow or operational problem, then uses the right combination of AI, automation, rules, integrations, software, and human judgment to improve how that work gets done.

Projects are scoped around a defined responsibility, built with the business's systems and constraints in mind, tested before launch, and handed over with clear ownership. Optional continuation can be scoped when additional maintenance, optimization, enhancement, or managed responsibility is actually useful.

AI should earn its place in the workflow. Where a rule, system control, or human decision is more dependable, that is what should be used.
