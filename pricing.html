Pricing

Fixed project pricing for defined production work.

Palomma prices production implementations around the operational responsibility being delivered—not around hours, a generic technology package, or an arbitrary “starting at” number.

  • Scope reflects the workflow responsibility and required outcome.
  • Systems, integrations, data conditions, approvals, controls, and production requirements affect the quote.
  • The proposal defines what is included, what the customer provides, and what constitutes acceptance and handoff.

No public project minimums yet

Palomma is not publishing numeric implementation bands until a complete minimum engagement and delivery economics are sufficiently proven. A workflow can vary materially depending on systems, data, authority, and production requirements.

When a separate scoping fee may apply

If material uncertainty prevents a responsible production quote, Palomma may first scope the workflow, integrations, data, architecture, or acceptance requirements as a bounded fixed-fee engagement. It is used only when the uncertainty itself requires real investigation.

When a prototype may apply

A prototype or proof may be scoped when there is a specific technical feasibility question to answer before production. It is not positioned as a cheap starter product or substitute for a production implementation.

After launch

The implementation includes bounded stabilization and handoff. Enhancements, maintenance, tuning, or managed operation can be scoped separately when there is an ongoing responsibility to perform. A mandatory retainer is not required.
