What We Build

Start with the workflow problem.

Palomma builds custom AI-enabled operational systems around recurring work that is still manual, fragmented, or difficult to scale. The technology is chosen around the work—not the other way around.

Quoting & Commercial Response

Bring requirements, product or service information, pricing inputs, approvals, and proposal preparation into a more reliable workflow.

Intake & Document Operations

Turn incoming emails, forms, files, and requests into organized information that can be checked, routed, and moved into the right system.

Orders & Back-Office Workflows

Connect repetitive administrative steps, lookups, approvals, system updates, and handoffs behind recurring operational work.

Sales & Commercial Workflows

Improve the work around CRM, account research, preparation, record updates, information flow, and commercial coordination.

The supporting capabilities come second.

A solution may use AI agents, retrieval, automation, integrations, deterministic rules, custom software, or human approvals. Palomma combines only the pieces needed to improve the operational responsibility being solved.

Not every step should be AI. AI belongs where judgment, interpretation, language, or flexible reasoning helps. Rules, system controls, and human decisions stay in the workflow where they are more reliable.

Good fit

Recurring work

The same kind of operational work happens repeatedly across people, documents, systems, or approvals.

Fragmented execution

People are repeatedly reading, copying, checking, routing, looking up, preparing, or re-entering information.

Defined responsibility

There is a real operational outcome to improve—not simply a request to “add AI.”

Existing systems matter

The solution needs to work with the tools, data, controls, and people already involved in the process.
